### Projects

**[אצבע · Etzba](https://etzba.ilanox.dev)**
Shift tracker and pay calculator for hourly workers in Israel. Tap in when a shift starts, tap out when it ends, and see your pay by Israeli labour law: overtime, Shabbat, holidays and net pay. Android and web, works offline, no account.
